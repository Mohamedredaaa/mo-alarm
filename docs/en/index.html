Quick Answers & Setup

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, offline accuracy, ultra-low battery usage, and alarm setup.

Is Mo Alarm really 100% free and completely ad-free?
Yes. Mo Alarm is 100% free fi sabilillah with zero banner ads, zero video interruptions before or after Adhan, zero paid subscriptions, and zero analytics tracking.
Is Mo Alarm light on battery and completely private offline?
Yes, 100%. Mo Alarm calculates prayer times locally on your phone without constant internet polling—preventing battery drain or overheating—and stores all data strictly on your device.
Do prayer times, Qibla compass, and Adhan alarms work offline without internet?
Yes. Astronomical prayer times, Qibla bearing toward Makkah, Hijri dates, wake-up challenges, and all 121 Adhkar work 100% offline anywhere in the world.
How does the Fajr alarm wake up heavy sleepers?
You can require solving Math equations, shaking your phone vigorously, or completing Morning Dhikr taps to stop the alarm, backed by progressive 25%→100% volume and a 5-minute post-Fajr awake verification check—making it a complete ad-free Islamic alternative to Alarmy.
How does Mosque Auto-Silent work during daily Salah and Friday Jumu'ah?
Once enabled, Mo Alarm automatically switches your phone to Do Not Disturb / Silent mode at prayer times (10–60 mins) and Friday Khutbah (20–90 mins), then restores your previous ringer volume automatically.
How do I prevent delayed Adhan notifications on Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, or Huawei?
On first launch, Mo Alarm displays a one-tap setup card to grant Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) and Unrestricted Battery usage so Samsung One UI, Xiaomi HyperOS/MIUI, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, and Huawei never delay an Adhan or Fajr alarm.
How do I install Mo Alarm on Android (APK) or iPhone (iOS 17+ IPA) for free?
On Android, download app-release.apk and open it (if Play Protect prompts for independent GitHub APKs, tap More details → Install anyway). On iPhone, download MoAlarm-iOS.ipa and sideload it for free using Sideloadly or AltStore with your regular Apple ID.
Which prayer calculation methods and Muezzin voices are included?
Mo Alarm supports Umm Al-Qura (Makkah), Egyptian General Authority of Survey, Muslim World League (MWL), ISNA, Karachi, Gulf/Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, North Africa, and Turkish Diyanet (both Standard and Hanafi Asr), plus 292 Adhan recordings and automatic Post-Adhan Dua.
Does Mo Alarm calculate Tahajjud (last third of the night) and Sunnah fasting days?
Yes. It automatically calculates the shifting last third of the night between Maghrib and Fajr for Qiyam al-Layl, and sends evening reminders for Mondays & Thursdays, White Days (13–15 Hijri), Ashura, and Arafah with one-tap Suhoor alarms.
How do I receive future app updates?
Mo Alarm includes a lightweight GitHub Releases update checker that alerts you inside the app whenever a new version is published so you can update in one tap without losing settings or streaks.
